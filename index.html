HOW TO PLAY

TOILET TROUBLE

Find a qualifying public restroom somewhere inside the search zone around Union Square.

  1. Start at a random location.Press Begin Search and the game places you somewhere inside the dashed search zone.
  2. Move with WASD only.W = north, A = west, S = south, D = east. Each press moves you 25 meters. Arrow keys pan the map.
  3. Follow the clues.The compass points toward the nearest qualifying restroom. Hot-and-cold feedback tells you whether you are getting closer.
  4. Find the restroom.Get within 45 meters of a qualifying location to win. The stopwatch will start when you press Begin Search

CSV data: locations come from the provided public-restroom dataset. Verify current hours before visiting.
